JavaScript: I used JavaScript to make the homepage's "What do I build?" button reveal a
hidden message, to auto-update the footer's copyright year on every page, and to

page reload.

Bootstrap: I used Bootstrap's navbar component (with a responsive collapsible menu),
card component for the projects grid, accordion component on the about page, and
form styling classes on the contact page.
